# Historical manuscript — provenance only

The files in this folder are unchanged copies of the original manuscript supplied for the mathematical review:

- `manuscrito.pdf`: the original English PDF, 27 physical pages, including a highlights page followed by 26 scientific pages.
- `manuscrito.tex`: the historical source identified as **M** in the mathematical audit.
- `main_energia_elsevier.tex`: the historical alternative entry point identified as **F** in the audit.
- `reference.bib`: the original bibliography, preserved for provenance rather than endorsed as a verified reading list.

The two original sources have the same scientific body; the audit explains their four-line offset and other packaging differences. Their filenames, line coordinates, comments and content are preserved so the collaborator can locate the original statements. Do not substitute their withdrawn claims for the corrected definitions and proofs in `../manuscript/`.

These source copies are included for reading and line-based auditing, not as a separately complete historical build project. Their original figure assets and build environment are not included here; the PDF already shows the historical figures. No reconstruction of the old document is necessary to implement the new protocol.

The historical training notebook and CSV results are available separately from the repository identified in `../HISTORICAL_CODE_AUDIT.md`. They are not new benchmark outputs, and the notebook is not bundled or executed by this package. Claims about missing checkpoints mean they were not present in the inspected versioned tree; uninspected private files are not presumed to exist.
